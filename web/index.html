A small place to trade skills

Find someone who can teach you, and learn something from you too.

I built SkillSwap because most “learning” sites feel like shouting into a crowd. Here you just say what you can help with, what you want to learn, and the site shows people who actually line up with that.

How it works

1

Make a profile

Name, email, one skill you can share, one thing you want to learn. That’s enough.

2

We look for overlap

A perfect match is a two-way swap. If you only share a skill or a goal, we still show that.

3

Reach out

Matches include an email, so you can say hello and decide how you want to learn together.
